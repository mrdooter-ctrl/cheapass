FAQ

Which smart plugs measure power use?

The Kasa KP115 and KP125M show live watts and total energy. The Amazon Smart Plug only switches on and off.

Do Kasa plugs work with Home Assistant?

Yes. Home Assistant has a Kasa integration that talks to the plugs on your local network. The KP125M also works over Matter.

Smart plug or Kill A Watt?

The Kill A Watt has a screen and no Wi-Fi, app or account. It's best for a quick check of one device. A smart plug logs use over time and can switch things off on a schedule.

Is a surge protector strip enough for a NAS?

No. A strip absorbs spikes. It does nothing when the power cuts mid-write. A NAS needs a small UPS.
