FAQ

Is a GaN charger worth it over the one in the box?

Yes, if your phone came without one or you carry a laptop. GaN chargers run cooler and are about half the size of the old silicon kind at the same wattage.

What wattage do I need?

30W charges any phone at full speed and most tablets. 45W covers a MacBook Air or a Steam Deck. A 15 inch work laptop wants 65W or more, which usually costs more than $30.

Does a 240W cable work with a 30W charger?

Yes. The cable rating is a ceiling, not a demand. The charger and the device agree on the speed, and the cable only has to handle it.

Braided or plain?

Braided lasts longer when it gets bent, stepped on or stuffed in a bag. On a desk where it never moves, plain is fine.
