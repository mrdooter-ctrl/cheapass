Which one?

  • UC250: the sensible one. Two drive bays, two SSD slots, an Intel N150 and 2.5GbE. Right for a first NAS.
  • UT2: a NAS you can carry, with a battery inside. Built for photographers dumping cards on the road. Takes SSDs, not hard drives.
