FAQ

NVMe or SATA M.2: which enclosure do I need?

Look at the notches on the drive's connector. One notch (M key) is usually NVMe. Two notches (B+M key) is usually SATA. The Sabrent EC-SNVE, the UGREEN NVMe and SATA case and the dockteck take both, so they're the safe buy when you're not sure.

How fast is a 10Gbps enclosure?

About 1,000 MB/s in real copies. That's slower than the drive is inside a PC, and about twice what a SATA SSD manages over USB.

Do these enclosures get hot?

The drive does, during long copies. Aluminum shells spread the heat. Expect speeds to dip on very long writes. For normal backups you won't notice.

Can I use one as a boot drive for a mini PC?

Yes. Most mini PCs boot from USB. Flash the installer onto the SSD in the enclosure and pick it in the boot menu.
