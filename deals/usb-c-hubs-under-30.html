FAQ

Will a cheap USB-C hub charge my laptop?

Only if it has a USB-C power delivery port. The Anker 332 and both Revodoks pass your charger through. The hub keeps a little for itself, so expect a bit less than the charger's rating. The UGREEN 6-in-1 and the Anker 4-port do not charge.

Do these hubs run a monitor at 4K 60Hz?

Some do. The UGREEN 6-in-1 and the Revodok 7-in-1 list 4K at 60Hz. Others list 4K at 30Hz, which is fine for documents and choppy for games. Check the listing before you buy.

Do they work with a mini PC or a Mac mini?

Yes. Data ports work on any USB-C port. Video needs a USB-C port that carries DisplayPort, which most mini PCs and every recent Mac have.

Hub or dock?

A hub is small, runs off the laptop and travels. A dock has its own power brick, more ports and a price well above $30. For one screen and a few USB devices, a hub is enough.
