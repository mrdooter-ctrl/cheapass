FAQ

Will a 2.5GbE USB adapter work over my existing Cat5e cables?

Usually yes for normal home runs. You do need a 2.5GbE switch or a 2.5GbE port on your NAS or router on the other end to see speeds above 1Gbps.

Do Realtek RTL8156 adapters work on Linux and Proxmox?

Modern Linux kernels include the r8152 driver out of the box. Plugable and UGREEN adapters are widely used on Proxmox and Ubuntu homelabs.

Should I buy 2.5GbE or standard 1GbE?

If your NAS has a 2.5GbE port, spend a few extra dollars for 2.5GbE — backups finish more than twice as fast. Only pick 1GbE if you just need the cheapest spare wired port.
