FAQ

Why does a Pi need a high-endurance card?

Home Assistant writes to its database all day. Ordinary cards wear out under that and corrupt without warning. Endurance cards are built for cameras that write nonstop, which is the same job.

Should I boot Proxmox from a USB stick?

Use the stick to install Proxmox, then put Proxmox on an SSD. Proxmox writes logs constantly and can wear out a stick. Unraid is the exception: it is made to boot from USB.

What size do I need?

64GB is plenty for Home Assistant on a Pi. A boot stick for Unraid or an installer needs less than 32GB, but 128GB costs almost the same.

Is a cheap card a fake?

Buy from the listing sold by the brand or by Amazon itself. Fake cards report a size they do not have. Run a free tool like H2testw or F3 on a new card before you trust it.
