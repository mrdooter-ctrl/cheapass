FAQ

Velcro ties or zip ties?

Velcro. You can undo and reuse them, and you never cut a cable by accident when you snip one off.

Will adhesive cable clips damage my desk?

They can pull paint or veneer off when removed. Clean the spot with rubbing alcohol before sticking, and peel slowly when you take them off.

Does an under-desk tray need drilling?

Not the clamp-on kind. Check how thick your desk edge is before you buy, since the clamp has a maximum.

How do I hide cords running down a wall?

An adhesive raceway like the Delamu kit. Cut it to length, stick it on, and the cords snap inside.
