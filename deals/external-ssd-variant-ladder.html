FAQ

Should I buy 1TB, 2TB or 4TB?

1TB is fine for daily documents and a single laptop. 2TB is usually the best price per TB for photos, 4K video and Proxmox backups. 4TB makes sense when you want one rugged drive to hold an entire off-site NAS copy.

Samsung T7 Shield or Crucial X9 Pro?

Both hit ~1,000MB/s over USB 3.2 Gen2 and hold sustained write speeds well. T7 Shield has a thicker IP65 rubber bumper; Crucial X9 Pro is smaller in an aluminum shell. Pick whichever is cheaper in your capacity today.

Are portable SSDs okay for long-term cold storage?

Yes for active rotating backups plugged in every month or two. Do not leave any flash SSD unpowered in a drawer for years without refreshing it.
