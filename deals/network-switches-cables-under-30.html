FAQ

Unmanaged or smart switch?

Unmanaged means plug in and done. A smart switch like the TL-SG105E adds a web page with VLANs, QoS and port mirroring. If you don't know what a VLAN is yet, unmanaged is fine.

Will a switch slow down my internet?

No. A gigabit switch adds no noticeable delay. It just gives the router more ports.

Cat6 or Cat5e?

Both do gigabit. Cat6 costs about the same and handles 10 gigabit on short runs, so buy Cat6.

Do I need a 2.5 gigabit switch?

Only if your NAS and your PC both have 2.5 gigabit ports and you move big files often. Those switches cost more than $30. Gigabit is plenty for streaming and backups.
