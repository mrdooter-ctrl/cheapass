FAQ

ZBDongle-P or ZBDongle-E for Home Assistant?

Pick ZBDongle-P (TI CC2652P) if you plan to run Zigbee2MQTT — it has the longest track record. Pick ZBDongle-E (Silicon Labs EFR32MG21) if you want ZHA or plan to experiment with Thread/Matter firmware.

Why do I need a USB 2.0 extension cable for a Zigbee stick?

USB 3.0 ports and SSDs radiate 2.4GHz noise that swamps Zigbee signals. A cheap 6-foot USB 2.0 extension moves the radio away from the mini PC and stops sensors from dropping offline.

Does the TP-Link UB500 work with Home Assistant?

Yes. It uses a Realtek chipset supported by Linux and Home Assistant's built-in Bluetooth integration for local BLE sensors.
