FAQ

Will a VESA mount fit my mini PC?

Most mini PCs from Beelink, GMKtec, Intel NUC and Minisforum come with VESA holes or a plate. Adjustable cradles like the AVLT hold boxes without holes, up to the thickness in the listing.

Does my monitor have VESA holes?

Look at the back for four screw holes in a square, 75 mm or 100 mm apart. Most office monitors have them. Many curved and very thin monitors do not.

Does a cooling pad help a mini PC?

It helps a box that pulls air in from the bottom. Raising it and adding a slow fan drops temperatures and fan noise. It does little for a box that breathes from the sides.

Won't a fan make it louder?

A large fan at low speed is quieter than a small fan screaming inside the case. The AC Infinity has a speed dial, so you can turn it down until you stop hearing it.
