FAQ

Do I really need a precision screwdriver to upgrade a mini PC?

Yes. M.2 SSD screws are tiny and strip easily with a household Phillips screwdriver. A proper precision bit kit saves you from ruining a brand-new board.

Will a tall M.2 heatsink fit inside a mini PC?

Not always. Measure the lid clearance first. Full-height heatsinks fit desktops and some NAS bays; low-profile pads or flat plates fit tight mini PCs.

When is repasting a mini PC worth it?

If the fan ramps loudly at light load after a year or two of 24/7 uptime, fresh MX-6 or NT-H1 often drops temps by several degrees.
