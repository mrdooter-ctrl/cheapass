FAQ

USB switch or full HDMI KVM?

If your monitor already has two video inputs (HDMI + DisplayPort), a cheap USB 3.0 switch handles keyboard, mouse and webcam for less money. Buy a full HDMI KVM when you want one button to flip both the screen and USB gear together.

Will a USB switch power a busy webcam or audio interface?

Look for a switch with an optional USB-C power input (like the UGREEN or Cable Matters models) so high-draw peripherals never drop out when switching.

Does a 2-port HDMI KVM add input lag?

No noticeable lag for desk work, coding or homelab management. Competitive high-refresh gamers should stick to a USB-only switch and plug DisplayPort straight into the monitor.
