FAQ

How many joules do I need?

For a desk or a small homelab, 700 joules or more is a sensible floor. More joules means it can absorb more surges before it wears out. No strip stops a direct lightning strike.

When should I replace a surge strip?

When its protection light goes out, after a big surge, or after five or so years. Most keep passing power after the protection is used up, which is why the light matters.

What does a watt meter tell me?

Plug it between the wall and a device and it shows the watts it draws right now and the kilowatt hours over time. Multiply by your electricity rate to see what it costs a month.

Can I plug a surge strip into another strip?

No. Daisy-chaining strips is a fire risk and breaks most warranties. Buy one strip with enough outlets.
