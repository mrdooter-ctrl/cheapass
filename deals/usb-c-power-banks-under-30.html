FAQ

10,000mAh or 20,000mAh?

10,000mAh fits in a pocket and recharges most phones 1.5 to 2 times. 20,000mAh weighs roughly twice as much but covers a weekend trip or two phones at once.

Can a 22.5W or 30W bank charge a laptop?

30W will slowly top up a MacBook Air or Steam Deck when idle, but for real laptop charging under load step up to the 65W 20,000mAh tier.

Are 20,000mAh banks allowed on airplanes?

Yes. 20,000mAh (~74Wh) is well under the FAA/TSA 100Wh carry-on limit. Always keep power banks in carry-on luggage, never checked bags.
