FAQ

Why not just buy the cheapest 425VA UPS for my NAS?

Most 425VA entry models skip the USB data port, so your NAS cannot detect a power outage and shut down automatically. Step up to 600VA (like APC BE600M1) or higher for USB graceful shutdown.

Do I need pure sinewave (1500VA)?

Not for a 2-bay NAS or mini PC with an external power brick. Pure sinewave (like CyberPower CP1500PFCLCD) matters if you run a tower server, workstation PSU with active PFC, or multiple servers on one unit.

How long does a UPS battery last?

Usually 3 to 5 years before the lead-acid cell needs a cheap replacement battery. The electronics last much longer.
