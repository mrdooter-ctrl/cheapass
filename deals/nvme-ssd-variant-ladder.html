FAQ

Crucial P3 Plus or WD_BLACK SN850X for Proxmox?

Pick the WD_BLACK SN850X for Proxmox, Home Assistant databases or NAS caching — its TLC flash and DRAM cache handle nonstop random writes far better than QLC drives like the P3 Plus.

Will a 4TB NVMe fit inside a mini PC?

Check whether your mini PC accepts double-sided M.2 2280 drives. Crucial P3 Plus is single-sided and fits almost anything; 4TB SN850X is double-sided.

Do I need a separate heatsink?

Most mini PCs include a thermal pad on the lid or have tight clearance. Only add a tall M.2 heatsink when you have room inside a desktop or NAS bay.
