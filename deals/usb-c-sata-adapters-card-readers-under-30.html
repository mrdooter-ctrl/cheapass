FAQ

Will a USB-to-SATA cable power a big 3.5-inch desktop hard drive?

No. Bus-powered cables only run 2.5-inch SATA SSDs and laptop drives. A 3.5-inch NAS drive needs an enclosure or dock with a 12V wall plug.

USB-to-SATA cable or full drive enclosure?

Get a bare cable if you swap, clone or wipe multiple drives on a desk. Get a full enclosure if one drive will stay plugged in permanently.

Why buy a dedicated SD card reader?

Cheap built-in laptop slots are slow or missing altogether. A USB 3.0 reader cuts Home Assistant and Raspberry Pi image flashing time in half.
