FAQ

Cat6 or Cat6a?

Cat6 handles gigabit and 2.5GbE at any home distance, and 10GbE on short runs. Cat6a is rated for 10GbE up to 100 m. For patching inside a rack, either works.

Do I need shielded cable?

Only near strong electrical noise, like motors or power cables run alongside. At home, plain Cat6 is fine. Shielded cable needs grounded jacks to do any good.

What is a keystone jack?

The socket that snaps into a wall plate or patch panel. You punch the cable's wires into the back, then plug a patch cable into the front.

Why buy a tester?

When a cable you crimped or punched down does not work, a tester shows which wire is wrong in five seconds instead of an evening of guessing.
