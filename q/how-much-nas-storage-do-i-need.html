How much NAS storage do I need?

Less than the box can hold. More than you have today.

Three steps

  1. Add up what you have now. Photos, phone backups, laptop backups, videos. Check each device's storage settings and write the numbers down.
  2. Double it. That covers a few years of growth for most homes. Shoot a lot of 4K video? Triple it.
  3. Buy two drives of that size. In a 2-bay mirror you get the space of one drive. The second one is your safety net, not extra space.

Example: 1.5TB today, doubled is 3TB. Two 4TB drives in a mirror give you 4TB usable with room to spare.

Before you buy

  • Bigger drives are often cheaper per TB. If the next size up costs only a little more, take it. You'll fill it.
  • A mirror is not a backup. Keep a copy somewhere else. The cheap way.
  • Need more than two drives' worth? Look at a 4-bay box. See the NAS guide.
