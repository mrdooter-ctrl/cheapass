Fix barebone mini-PC M.2 NVMe clearance & thermal throttling

Stop buying chunky heatsinks and double-sided SSDs that trap the bottom cover open.

{{SEASON}}

Barebone GMKtec, Beelink, and Minisforum mini PCs leave razor-thin headroom over the M.2 2280 slots — often 0.5mm to 4mm total. Pre-installed desktop heatsinks won't close the lid, and stacked double-sided drives bend onto Wi-Fi cards.

60-second fitment checklist (before you open the chassis)

  1. Pick single-sided NAND: Use single-sided M.2 2280 drives (Crucial P3 Plus stays single-sided across 1TB, 2TB, and 4TB; WD_BLACK SN850X stays single-sided at 1TB/2TB for Proxmox/ZFS workloads).
  2. Match cooler thickness to real headroom: Under ~2mm clearance = flexible 0.5mm–1mm copper-graphene foil only (IcePC / JEYI). Over ~5mm clearance = slim aluminum block (be quiet! MC1 non-Pro).
  3. Never force M.2 standoff screws: Use an exact PH0/PH00 bit so you don't strip a captive mini-PC standoff.
  4. External bottom-plate airflow: If a closed Proxmox cluster runs hot, a silent 120mm USB fan underneath drops NVMe temps more than jamming oversized fins inside.
