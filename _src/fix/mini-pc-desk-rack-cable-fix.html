60-second layout fix (order of operations)

  1. Clear the desktop footprint: Put the mini PC behind your display (VESA cradle) or clamp it vertically under the desk edge.
  2. Stop adhesive failures: Drop heavy mini-PC power bricks and your flat-plug power strip into a mechanical clamp-on steel tray — tape sags once bricks warm up.
  3. Cut excess cord loops: Swap 6 ft/10 ft LAN & video leftovers for 3 ft Cat6 jumpers and a 3 ft HDMI lead.
  4. Standing-desk rule: Raise the desk to full max height first, then bundle the single wall drop into a zippered sleeve so lowering/raising never pulls DC jacks out.
