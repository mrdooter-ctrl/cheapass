On a shoot, the risk is a lost or dead memory card before you get home. A portable NAS keeps a second copy in the field until you're back at your desk.

Before you buy

  • It's SSD only. Fast and tough, but SSDs cost more per TB than hard drives. Size it for one trip, not your whole archive.
  • It is not your archive. At home, the photos belong on a real NAS with a backup somewhere else.
  • Not a Plex box. The chip is built for files and photos, not for converting video.
