NAS or mini PC: which one do you need?

A NAS is a closet. A mini PC is a worker. Most homes end up with both.

The short answer

  • Mostly backups and photos? A NAS. Two drives, mirrored, done.
  • Mostly apps, like Home Assistant, Pi-hole or Plex with a small library? A mini PC with a USB drive.
  • Big media library and Plex for the family? One NAS with an Intel chip, or a cheap NAS plus a cheap mini PC. The second is often cheaper and easier to upgrade.

Why two boxes often beat one

  • Upgrades are cheaper. When apps need more power, you replace a mini PC, not the box holding your drives.
  • Fewer bad days. A crashed app doesn't take your files offline.
  • The catch: two boxes, two power plugs. See what they cost to run.
