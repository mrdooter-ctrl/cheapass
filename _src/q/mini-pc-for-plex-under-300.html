Best mini PC for Plex under $300

Short answer: any recent Intel box. The chip's video engine does the heavy lifting.

Plex only works hard when it has to convert video for a device that can't play the original. That's called transcoding. Intel chips have a built-in video engine (Quick Sync) that handles it with almost no effort. So under $300, pick Intel and stop worrying about cores.

The two picks

  • GMKtec NucBox G3 Ultra: ready to go, RAM and SSD included. Plug in, install Plex, done. Fine for a family streaming a few things at once.
  • GMKtec NucBox KB2 Max: more CPU for less money, but barebone. No RAM, no SSD. Only cheaper if you have spares in a drawer.

Before you buy

  • Plex needs Plex Pass to use the Intel video engine. Jellyfin does it free.
  • Your movies don't live on the mini PC. Its SSD is for the system. Keep media on a NAS or a USB drive (short on ports or wiring a NAS? See under-$30 USB-C hubs and gigabit switches and Cat6 cables).
  • If nobody transcodes, because every TV plays your files directly, the cheapest box on the list is plenty.
