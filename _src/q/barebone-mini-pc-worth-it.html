Is a barebone mini PC worth it?

Barebone means the box is cheap and the shopping list is not.

A barebone mini PC ships without RAM and without an SSD. The sticker price looks great. Then you buy the parts.

When barebone wins

  • You have spare parts. Old laptop RAM and an SSD from an upgrade. Then barebone is the cheapest real computer you can buy.
  • You want a lot of RAM. Ready-made boxes ship with a fixed amount. Barebone lets you buy what you need once, instead of upgrading later and keeping a useless stick.

When it doesn't

  • You have nothing to put in it. RAM plus an SSD usually closes most of the price gap with a ready-made box.
  • You don't want to open a computer. It's ten minutes with a small screwdriver. It's also ten minutes you didn't plan on.
