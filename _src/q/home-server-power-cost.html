What does a home server cost to run 24/7?

Usually less than one streaming subscription. Do the math once and stop wondering.

The formula

Watts x 24 x 365 / 1000 = kWh per year. Multiply by the rate on your electric bill.

Example: a box that averages 15 watts uses about 131 kWh a year. At 17 cents per kWh, that's about $22 a year.

Your numbers

Where the watts come from

  • Idle is what counts. A home server spends most of its life doing nothing. The idle number matters more than the maximum on the box.
  • Hard drives add up. Each spinning drive adds a few watts. SSDs add very little.
  • Measure it. A plug-in power meter costs less than a month of guessing.
