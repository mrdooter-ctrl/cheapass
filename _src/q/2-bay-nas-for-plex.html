Cheapest 2-bay NAS that can run Plex

Storing movies is easy. Playing them on every TV in the house is the hard part.

A lot of cheap NAS boxes use ARM chips. They're great at holding files and bad at converting video on the fly for a phone or an old TV. If you want Plex or Jellyfin on the NAS itself, you want an Intel chip.

The pick

  • UnifyDrive UC250: two drive bays, two NVMe slots and an Intel N150. It's the cheap 2-bay on our list with an Intel video engine. Drives not included.
