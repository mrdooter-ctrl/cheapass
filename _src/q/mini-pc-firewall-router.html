Mini PC as a router or firewall

The one spec that matters is the one people forget: two network ports.

OPNsense and pfSense turn a small PC into a router and firewall that's more flexible than the box your internet company gave you. It needs one port for the internet side and one for your home side. A USB network adapter technically works. It's also where the weird problems come from.

The pick

  • Beelink EQi Pro: two 2.5GbE ports, plenty of CPU, and enough RAM to run a few other things next to the firewall.

The other boxes on our list are good home servers, but we haven't confirmed two network ports on them. Check the brand's page before you plan on it.

Before you buy

  • Internet under 1 Gbps? Any port speed on this list keeps up.
  • You still need Wi-Fi. The firewall replaces the router part. Keep your old router in access point mode, or buy a separate access point.
  • Plan for downtime. When the firewall reboots, the whole house is offline. Do updates at night.
