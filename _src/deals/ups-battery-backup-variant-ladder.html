You don't need a massive battery to keep Plex running for hours — you just need 5 to 10 clean minutes plus a USB cable so your NAS shuts itself down safely. Pick the tier below that matches what's plugged into your wall.

What changes as you step up

  • 425VA (Router / Pi only): Keeps Wi-Fi and a Raspberry Pi alive through power blinks. No USB shutdown port.
  • 600VA–625VA (Sweet spot for 2-bay NAS + mini PC): Adds USB data signaling for automatic NAS/NUT shutdown.
  • 850VA (4-bay NAS + mini PC + switch): Extra headroom when spinning 4 drives plus a server.
  • 1500VA Pure Sinewave: For full racks, desktop towers, and active-PFC power supplies — includes live watt LCD readout.
