What changes as you step up

  • 1TB: Lowest entry cost. Best for work files, OS installers, or a single laptop backup.
  • 2TB (Sweet spot): Usually the lowest cost per TB on sale. Right size for photo libraries and homelab VM snapshots.
  • 4TB: Holds an entire off-site NAS copy on one rugged pocket drive without carrying a spinning hard drive.
