60-second diagnostic (check these first)

  1. Move the stick at least 1–2 meters away from the mini PC chassis, SSD enclosures, and power bricks using a USB 2.0 extension cable (not a blue USB 3.0 extension).
  2. Plug into a black USB 2.0 host port if your box has one — USB 2.0 ports do not generate 2.4GHz SuperSpeed RF hash. (No USB 2.0 port? Put a cheap passive USB 2.0 hub in front of the extension.)
  3. Separate Wi-Fi and Zigbee channels: Lock 2.4GHz Wi-Fi to channel 1 or 6 (20MHz width) and run Zigbee on channel 25 or 20.
  4. Add 2–3 mains-powered Zigbee routers (smart plugs/switches) between the coordinator and battery end-devices.

FAQ

Why do my Zigbee sensors keep dropping offline when plugged into a mini PC?

USB 3.0 ports, cables, and NVMe SSDs radiate broadband electromagnetic noise right in the 2.4GHz band used by Zigbee and Thread. Plugging a USB 3.0 extension cable does not fix it because the SuperSpeed data lines inside the cable leak noise too.

Should I pick ZBDongle-P or ZBDongle-E?

Pick ZBDongle-P (TI CC2652P) if you run Zigbee2MQTT or want the longest-proven coordinator. Pick ZBDongle-E (Silicon Labs EFR32MG21) if you want ZHA or plan to test Matter-over-Thread firmware.

What if my mini PC only has blue USB 3.0 ports?

Drop a cheap USB 2.0 hub (no USB 3.0 lanes inside) onto the host port, then run a 6 ft USB 2.0 extension cable out to the coordinator so no SuperSpeed lines run near the antenna.
