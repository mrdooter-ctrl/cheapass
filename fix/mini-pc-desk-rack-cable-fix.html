60-second layout fix (order of operations)

  1. Clear the desktop footprint: Put the mini PC behind your display (VESA cradle) or clamp it vertically under the desk edge.
  2. Stop adhesive failures: Drop heavy mini-PC power bricks and your flat-plug power strip into a mechanical clamp-on steel tray — tape sags once bricks warm up.
  3. Cut excess cord loops: Swap 6 ft/10 ft LAN & video leftovers for 3 ft Cat6 jumpers and a 3 ft HDMI lead.
  4. Standing-desk rule: Raise the desk to full max height first, then bundle the single wall drop into a zippered sleeve so lowering/raising never pulls DC jacks out.

FAQ

Can I VESA mount a mini PC if my monitor stand uses the VESA holes?

Not directly — you need either a dual-sided VESA extension bracket (like the VIVO or AVLT cradle mounted on the monitor arm pole/under-desk) or a hidden under-desk mount.

Why does double-sided tape fail with mini PC power bricks?

Mini PC AC-to-DC bricks are heavy and run warm, softening foam adhesive over weeks. Hang heavy power strips and bricks inside a clamp-on steel under-desk tray instead.

How do I bundle cables on a sit-stand desk without snapping DC plugs?

Raise the desk to its maximum standing height FIRST, then zip power + Ethernet into a flexible neoprene sleeve down one leg before lowering it.
