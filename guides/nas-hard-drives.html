The one spec that matters: CMR

NAS drives should be CMR, not SMR. SMR drives can crawl during a rebuild, which is exactly when you need them fast. WD Red Plus and Red Pro are CMR. Plain WD Red (no Plus) is not what you want in a NAS.

Red Plus or Red Pro?

  • Red Plus: for home boxes up to about 8 bays. Quieter at the small sizes.
  • Red Pro: 7200 RPM, bigger sizes, longer warranty. Louder. Worth it at 16TB and up, where it's often the cheaper drive per TB anyway.

Buying tips

  • Mirror? Buy two of the same size. You only get the space of one.
  • Don't buy all drives from one batch if you can help it. Drives from the same batch can fail close together.
  • Noise: if the NAS lives in your office, 7200 RPM drives will remind you.
