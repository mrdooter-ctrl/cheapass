Cheap mini PCs for a home server

An old laptop with ambitions. That's what you're buying.

A mini PC uses a fraction of the power of a tower and runs Home Assistant, Pi-hole, Plex or a few virtual machines without complaint. Pick by the job, not by the spec sheet.

Pick by the job

  • Home Assistant, Pi-hole, small stuff: the cheapest box here is plenty.
  • Plex or Jellyfin for the family: an Intel chip. Intel's built-in video engine (Quick Sync) handles transcoding without breaking a sweat. Plex needs Plex Pass for that. Jellyfin does it free.
  • Proxmox with several VMs: more cores and more RAM. RAM runs out before the CPU does.

Before you buy

  • Barebone means no RAM and no SSD. Cheaper up front, but budget for RAM and an SSD on top.
  • Check the network port. 2.5GbE is nice if your NAS has it too.
  • Mini PCs are loud under load on some models. Fine in a closet, less fine on a desk.

Storage for media goes on a NAS, not the mini PC's little SSD.
