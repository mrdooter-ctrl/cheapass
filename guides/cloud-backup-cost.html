Cloud backup: pay once or pay yearly?

A lifetime plan is a bet that you and the company both stick around.

Cloud backup is the copy that survives a fire, a flood or a burglar who likes NAS boxes. There are two kinds of deal, and they cover different things.

"Per year, kept 5 yrs" divides a lifetime price by five years so you can compare it to a yearly plan.

What each one is for

  • Backblaze Personal backs up one computer with no storage cap. It does not back up a NAS or network drives. For a laptop full of photos, it's hard to beat.
  • pCloud lifetime is storage you upload to. Pay once. It only wins if you keep it for years. At 2TB it pays for itself in about five years against $99 a year.

The honest math

  • Under five years? Yearly is cheaper.
  • Lifetime means the company's lifetime, not yours. Spread the risk: keep a second copy at home.
  • Backing up a NAS needs an object storage service your NAS supports, not a personal plan. Use the calculator to price it.
